## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-craft fusion of Classical Chinese Aesthetics (现代中式美学) and precise hardware-grade digital product architecture. Inspired by Xuan paper mounting traditions, cinnabar seal impressions (金石印章), and imperial jade artifacts, the interface translates ancient material culture into an ultra-sharp, tactile digital learning instrument.

### Visual Philosophy & Movements
- **Contemporary Chinese Elegance (新中式):** Quiet restraint, generous breathing room (留白, liúbái), delicate hairline borders mimicking lacquered tea boxes and mounting scrolls, accompanied by deep ink tones and warm Xuan paper canvases.
- **Hardware Component Architecture:** Precision industrial layout logic featuring double-bezel cards, milled nested radiuses, and physical tactile buttons with spring physics.
- **Atmospheric Depth:** Whisper-thin calligraphy watermarks embedded into module backgrounds with ghost-level opacities (1.5%–4%), providing cultural grounding without interfering with functional readability.

### Emotional Target
The experience conveys absolute linguistic mastery, quiet focus, scholarly authority, and tactile mechanical delight. It deliberately avoids gamification clichés like candy gradients or decorative emojis, treating the student as a calligrapher and linguistic craftsman.

## Colors

The color system is grounded in classical Chinese pigments and natural mineral tones, balanced against deep stone ink and textured warm paper.

### Functional Roles
- **Primary — Jade & Emerald (`#047857`, `#059669`, `#10b981`):** Represents growth, precision, correct inputs, accuracy, and scholastic advancement. Jade is the central navigational and active signal across interactive targets.
- **Secondary — Imperial Cinnabar (`#dc2626`, `#b91c1c`):** Utilized for seal stamp badges (Chop / 印章), Tone 4 descent indicators, critical alerts, and focal validation points.
- **Tertiary — Aged Amber & Streak Gold (`#d97706`, `#f59e0b`):** Guides streak counters, Tone 3 dipping vectors, mastery milestones, and warm warnings.
- **Celadon & Sky (`#0284c7`):** Allocated specifically to Tone 1 (High Flat 55) pitch contours and clear informational state capsules.
- **Neutrals — Ink & Paper:**
  - **Light Canvas (`#fbfbf9`, `#f4f4f0`):** Xuan-paper ivory, reducing eye fatigue over extended study sessions.
  - **Dark Canvas (`#09090b`):** Fine Hui ink-stick black (Zinc-950), strictly avoiding artificial `#000000`.
  - **Surfaces (`#ffffff` light, `#18181b` dark):** Pure ink stone and white porcelain substrates.

### Color Governance Rules
1. Never deploy high-saturation purple or synthetic magenta gradients.
2. Avoid saturated backgrounds; background hues must remain organic mineral tints or warm paper off-whites.
3. Every functional Mandarin tone contour adheres strictly to its assigned hue: Tone 1 (`#0284c7`), Tone 2 (`#059669`), Tone 3 (`#d97706`), Tone 4 (`#dc2626`), and Tone 5 Neutral (`#71717a`).

## Typography

The typographic stack creates clear differentiation across three semantic layers: Modern Geometric Display, Classical Hanzi Glyph Rendering, and Technical Acoustic Notation.

### Type Roles
- **Display & Headlines (`Outfit`):** Geometric, precise, and crisp. Uses negative letter-spacing (`-0.025em` to `-0.03em`) to anchor dashboards and module banners with structural gravitas.
- **Hanzi Glyphs (`notoSans` / Noto Sans SC):** Scaled with optical breathing room (`0.02em` to `0.05em` tracking). Glyphs are centered inside virtual square bounding boxes (九宫格 / 田字格 alignment principles) to preserve stroke balance.
- **Pinyin, Contours & Metrics (`JetBrains Mono`):** Exact monospaced rendering for diacritical tone markings, IME candidate indices, keyboard shortcuts (`[1-4]`, `[Space]`, `[Esc]`), and live WPM counters.

### Typographic Rhythm
- Maintain a strict vertical baseline alignment between Hanzi characters and their accompanying Pinyin tone representations. The Pinyin block always sits stacked immediately above the character with a standardized `0.25rem` micro-gap.
- Editorial Chinese story paragraphs feature generous line height (`1.8`–`2.0`) and subtle phrase boundary spacing to ensure smooth reading cadence.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model combines an architectural 12-column responsive fluid grid with the compositional balance of classical Chinese hanging scrolls.

### Spatial Structure
- **Max Content Bounding:** Centered content container capped at `1200px` to maintain focused line lengths and ergonomic scanning paths.
- **Grid Architecture:** 
  - **Desktop (≥ 1024px):** 12-column grid, `2rem` gutters, `2.5rem` edge margins.
  - **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column grid, `1.5rem` gutters, `1.5rem` edge margins.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column grid, `1rem` gutters, `1rem` edge margins.

### Nested Bezel Metric Rhythm
Interactive modules are structured through a nested double-bezel spacing rule:
- Outer casing: Fixed `0.375rem` (6px) padding enclosing the outer translucent bezel.
- Inner core cavity: Generous responsive inner padding ranging from `1.75rem` on mobile to `2.5rem` on desktop.
- Inter-module vertical rhythm: `3.5rem` to `5rem` separation to simulate discrete, framed museum artifacts.

## Elevation & Depth

Elevation is expressed through architectural layering, milled nested bezels, and whisper-soft ambient diffusion—avoiding high-contrast drops or heavy black casts.

### Elevation Levels

- **Surface Ground (Level 0):** The paper foundation (`#fbfbf9` / `#09090b`). No elevation.
- **Module Bezel (Level 1):** The outer rim of learning containers. Bound by a hairline border (`border: 1px solid rgba(228, 228, 231, 0.8)` in light mode, `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` in dark mode).
- **Core Raised Card (Level 2):** Floating inside the bezel with the signature whisper shadow:
  - `box-shadow: 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.03)`
  - Surface: Pristine white (`#ffffff`) or deep slate (`#18181b`).
- **Interactive / Hover Surface (Level 3):** Active flashcards, IME dropdown candidates, and modal sheets.
  - `box-shadow: 0 10px 25px -5px rgba(4, 120, 87, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.03)`
  - Transition: Spring physics (`cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`).
- **Overlays & Dialogs (Level 4):** SRS session modals, dictionary drawer:
  - Backing: Frosted Xuan blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` over `rgba(251, 251, 249, 0.85)`).
  - Shadow: Diffused ambient perimeter shadow (`0 25px 50px -12px rgba(9, 9, 11, 0.12)`).

### Background Watermark Integration
Each learning arena embeds an architectural Chinese watermark glyph (e.g. `字`, `打`, `合`, `句`, `听`, `读`, `考`):
- Scale: `240px` to `480px` font size.
- Placement: Right-bottom aligned, bleed outside container boundary.
- Opacity: Light mode `2.5%` (`rgba(9, 9, 11, 0.025)`), Dark mode `4%` (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`).
- Layer: Strictly `pointer-events: none` and `z-index: 0`.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary balances curved fluid ergonomics with precise inner geometry.

### Radius Hierarchy
- **Outer Shell Bezel:** `2.5rem` (40px) outer corner radius on desktop; `1.75rem` on mobile.
- **Nested Inner Core:** Math-matched radius (`calc(2.5rem - 0.375rem) = 2.125rem` / 34px) ensuring concentric curved lines with zero visual distortion.
- **Kinetic Buttons & Pills:** Fully rounded (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) creating aerodynamic tactile interaction capsules.
- **Seal Stamp Badges (Chop / 印章):** Sharp or micro-rounded square boundaries (`rounded-sm` / `4px`) referencing stone carvers' seals.
- **IME Candidates & Radical Blocks:** `0.5rem` to `0.75rem` for compact modular assembly.

## Components

### 1. Button-in-Button Kinetic CTAs
Primary call-to-actions follow a dual-capsule structure:
- **Outer Shell:** Pill-shaped container (`rounded-full`), padded with `pl-6 pr-2 py-2`, background Jade Base (`#059669`), hover state Jade Light (`#10b981`), text in crisp white `14px` bold.
- **Inner Kinetic Circle:** An enclosed circle (`h-9 w-9`) with `bg-white/20`, housing a Lucide vector arrow or play glyph. On button hover, the inner icon translates right by `2px` using spring curve `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`.
- **Keyboard Hint Capsule:** Monospaced shortcut hint (`[Enter]`, `[Space]`) displayed in a micro translucent pill inside secondary variants.

### 2. Double-Bezel Flashcard & Reading Containers
- **Outer Casing:** `rounded-[2.5rem] p-1.5 border border-zinc-200/80 dark:border-white/10 bg-zinc-100/50 dark:bg-zinc-900/40`.
- **Inner Slate:** `rounded-[2.125rem] bg-white dark:bg-zinc-900 p-8 sm:p-12 shadow-whisper relative overflow-hidden`.
- **Watermark Stamp:** Centered or bottom-anchored calligraphic character positioned behind content at ultra-low opacity.

### 3. Traditional Seal Badges (Chop Stamps / 印章)
- **Geometry:** Square or vertical rectangle (`rounded-[4px]`), enclosed by a `1.5px` border in Cinnabar (`#dc2626`).
- **Typography:** Classical serif or traditional Hanzi script in Cinnabar, accompanied by micro-metric JetBrains Mono notation (e.g. `HSK 1`, `LVL 08`).
- **Background:** Cinnabar ink wash tint (`rgba(220, 38, 38, 0.08)`).

### 4. Mathematical Chao Pitch Contour Visualizer
- Mandated vector component (`PitchContour.tsx`) showing exact pitch movement across five tonal levels:
  - Horizontal Tone 1 (High Level 55): Straight line in Celadon Sky (`#0284c7`).
  - Tone 2 (Rising 35): Ascending diagonal in Jade Base (`#059669`).
  - Tone 3 (Dipping 214): Smooth bezier dip-and-rise in Amber Streak (`#d97706`).
  - Tone 4 (Falling 51): Sharp drop in Cinnabar Stamp (`#dc2626`).
  - Tone 5 (Neutral 3): Centered circular node in Muted Ink (`#71717a`).

### 5. TypeRacer & IME Candidate Input
- **IME Matrix:** Horizontal candidate bar positioned beneath input with indices `1–5` in JetBrains Mono.
- **Active Selection:** Border highlight in Jade Base with translucent emerald fill (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.1)`).
- **Input Field:** Clean bottom border with subtle ghost outline, zero aggressive focus rings, accompanied by real-time stroke decomposition preview.

### 6. Iconography & Strict Anti-Pattern Rules
- **Vector Icons:** Strictly Lucide vector lines at `1.5px` or `1.75px` stroke weight.
- **Zero-Emoji Policy:** Emojis are strictly barred from UI copy, validation states, and system notifications. All status markers must use authentic Hanzi seals, Chao contour curves, or crisp vector indicators.
